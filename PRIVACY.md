# SpaceLens Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026

SpaceLens scans only folders and files you explicitly select. Analysis—including sizes, file types, modification dates, duplicate hashes, and cleanup recommendations—is performed locally on your device. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share filenames, file contents, scan results, analytics, advertising identifiers, or diagnostics.

Recent scan locations and preferences are stored locally. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple; the developer does not receive your payment-card details. SpaceLens does not use tracking technologies or third-party advertising SDKs.

You can remove local data by deleting the app. Questions may be submitted through the repository’s Support page.
