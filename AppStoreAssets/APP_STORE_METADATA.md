## Description

Storage Atlas turns storage analysis into a clear visual map. Scan a location you choose, understand what is consuming space, investigate the biggest opportunities, and build a cleanup basket before anything is moved.

SEE YOUR STORAGE

• Explore folders through an interactive sunburst or treemap
• Compare logical and physical space with detailed size breakdowns
• Navigate from a high-level overview into individual folders and files
• Understand file-type composition with clear categories and proportional indicators

FIND WHAT MATTERS

• Largest Files highlights the items with the greatest storage impact
• Duplicate Files verifies matching content with SHA-256 instead of relying only on names
• Old & Unopened helps locate material that may no longer be useful
• Dev Junk surfaces common build outputs and caches for developers

CLEAN WITH CONTROL

Storage Atlas does not turn a recommendation into deletion. Add candidates to a cleanup basket, review the selection and reclaimed-space estimate, then move confirmed items to Trash. Built-in undo helps you recover from a change of mind.

FOR EVERYDAY USERS

Find forgotten downloads, oversized videos, repeated documents, and folders that quietly grew over time. Storage Atlas makes a complex disk understandable without requiring technical knowledge.

FOR PROFESSIONALS

Inspect project trees, locate build artifacts, distinguish physical allocation from logical file size, verify duplicates by content, and analyze external or user-selected locations. It is designed for developers, photographers, video teams, researchers, and anyone managing large working sets.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Scanning and analysis happen locally. Storage Atlas reads only the folders you authorize and does not transmit filenames, file contents, hashes, or scan results to the developer.

PRO SUBSCRIPTION

Storage Atlas is free to download and use. Scanning, visualization, cleanup, and export stay free forever, with no account and no sign-in. Storage Atlas Pro unlocks the advanced layer: the menu-bar space watcher and storage trends over time.
